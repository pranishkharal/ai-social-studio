# Caption Guide — example

Copy this file to `caption_guide.md` (same folder) and replace the rules
below with your own caption guide. The caption generator reads
`caption_guide.md` when it writes post captions for a clip, so your guide
is the knowledge it follows — the same way you would paste a guide into a
chat with an AI and ask it for captions.

`caption_guide.md` is git-ignored: your guide stays on your machine and is
never pushed to GitHub. This example file is safe to share.

---

## What a good post caption does

- Hooks the viewer in the first second.
- Makes them stay to see what happened.
- Baits them into checking the comments.
- Makes them want to share or argue.

## Rules

- Name the specific moment, not the game in general. "GTA 6" alone is not
  a caption; "the $500 strawberry farmer" is.
- Viral moments get FULL context: streamer, game, the specific thing, and
  the number if there is one.
- A mystery variant drops the key detail ("what this farmer sells ONE
  strawberry for") to pull comments.
- Capitalize the reaction word: SPEECHLESS, SHOCKED, PROVED, REVEALED.
- Keep it to one line. Hashtags are added separately at post time.
- Never invent a reaction the streamer did not have. If the clip has no
  clear reaction, use the moment itself, not a fake emotion.
- Keep captions PG: no insults, threats, or slurs — a caption carrying
  one gets no views.

## Caption Templates

Starter formats for common streamer moments. The generator picks the
template that matches the moment in the clip: it reads the heading
(`If ...:`), then the quoted template lines under it. `(streamer)` is
the streamer's display name; `(quick context)` is the specific thing
in the clip. Templates carry their own emojis.

If the Streamer Meets Someone Famous:
"the moment (streamer) had no clue who that was ❤️‍🩹"
Tip: Don't name the person — let the comments guess.

If They React to a Song:
"the moment (streamer) heard that new song and lost it 😭❤️‍🩹"
Tip: Don't name the artist — the mystery pulls comments.

If It's an Emotional Moment:
"the moment (streamer) got emotional on stream ❤️‍🩹"
"(streamer) was crushed after (quick context) ❤️‍🩹"
Tip: Keep the vibe dramatic.

If They Apologize:
"the moment (streamer) owned up and said sorry ❤️‍🩹"
Tip: Owning a mistake is rare — frame it that way.

If They Have a Big Career Moment:
"(streamer) just had the biggest moment of his career ❤️‍🩹"
Tip: Make the moment feel as big as it is.

## Trigger Words

PROVED, SPEECHLESS, REGRETTED, REVEALED.
Capitalize the most important words in a caption to catch the eye.
